About

Casablanca
Arbitration Days

Hosted by Casablanca Finance City Authority in partnership with CIMAC – Casablanca International Mediation and Arbitration Centre, this landmark event will explore Morocco's evolving position in the global dispute resolution landscape.

Over two days, leading practitioners, policymakers, institutional representatives and academics will discuss:

  • Legislative developments in arbitration and mediation
  • Institutional innovation and global best practices
  • Morocco's ambition as a regional arbitration hub
  • Investment, legal reform and dispute resolution

This 10-year milestone reflects CIMAC's continued commitment to positioning Morocco as a competitive international dispute resolution seat.
